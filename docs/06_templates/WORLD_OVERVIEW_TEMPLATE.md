# World Overview Template

> **Purpose:** This template guides the creation of a project-specific world overview.
>
> A world overview describes the fictional setting in which the game takes place. It establishes the world's identity, defining characteristics, geography, history, societies, cultures, factions, important locations, and internal rules.
>
> It provides a shared reference for narrative design, quest design, level design, character creation, environmental storytelling, and world-related gameplay systems.
>
> This document is a high-level world reference. Detailed character histories, location specifications, faction structures, quest narratives, and regional descriptions should be maintained in separate documents when necessary.
>
> AI agents must preserve established canon, distinguish confirmed information from proposals, and avoid inventing major world facts without appropriate approval.

## 17. AI-Assisted Worldbuilding Guidelines

AI agents may assist with worldbuilding research, organization, consistency checks, and document generation.

They MUST:

1. Read the approved Game Vision, GDD, Narrative Overview, and relevant project design documents before generating world content.
2. Inspect existing world, location, character, faction, and historical documents.
3. Preserve established canon and continuity.
4. Distinguish confirmed facts from interpretations and proposals.
5. Never invent major historical events, cultures, political structures, or world rules and present them as established facts.
6. Avoid contradictions between regions, factions, characters, and historical events.
7. Respect the project's intended setting, tone, and player experience.
8. Use credible research sources when real-world history, geography, or cultural information is involved.
9. Distinguish real historical information from fictional adaptations.
10. Avoid stereotypes and oversimplified cultural representations.
11. Avoid creating unnecessary worldbuilding that does not support the game's experience.
12. Reference detailed specifications rather than duplicating their contents.
13. Identify assumptions, uncertainties, and unresolved decisions.
14. Request project owner approval for major changes to established world canon.
15. Never mark proposed content as approved without explicit authorization.

### 17.1 AI Worldbuilding Review Checklist

Before generating or modifying worldbuilding content, verify:

- [ ] The content supports the game's vision.
- [ ] It is consistent with the established setting.
- [ ] Historical and geographical claims are appropriately supported.
- [ ] Cultural representations have adequate context.
- [ ] Important characters and factions remain consistent.
- [ ] Geography and location relationships are coherent.
- [ ] World rules do not contradict existing rules.
- [ ] Gameplay implications are realistic for the implemented systems.
- [ ] New content has a clear purpose.
- [ ] Canon classifications are accurate.
- [ ] Unresolved decisions are identified.
- [ ] Related documents are updated when necessary and authorized.

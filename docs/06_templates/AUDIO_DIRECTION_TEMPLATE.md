This document defines the audio identity of **[Game Name]**.

It establishes the musical direction, sound design principles, environmental audio characteristics, voice and dialogue approach, and auditory feedback conventions that guide the game's audio development.

It serves as a reference for:

- Music composition and selection.
- Sound effects design and production.
- Environmental ambience.

- Audio consistency reviews.

This document covers the creative and experiential direction of the game's audio.

It does not replace:

- Audio asset standards.
- Audio production pipeline.
- Technical audio implementation guidelines.
- Individual music and sound effect specifications.
- Dialogue scripts and localization documentation.
- Gameplay and narrative design documents.

# 13. AI-Assisted Audio Production

AI tools may support audio concept exploration, reference generation, sound design experimentation, music prototyping, and voice production where appropriate.

AI-generated output must follow these requirements:

1. Use this document as the authoritative audio direction reference.
2. Provide relevant project-specific requirements when generating audio.
3. Do not assume AI-generated output automatically matches the approved audio direction.
4. Review generated assets for quality, consistency, and technical suitability.
5. Verify licensing, usage rights, and applicable tool restrictions.
6. Respect consent, authorization, and applicable restrictions when using synthetic voices or imitating identifiable voices.
7. Do not introduce major audio direction changes without project owner approval.
8. Preserve approved references and production decisions.
9. Do not use generated references as a replacement for actual audio asset specifications.

## 13.1 AI Audio Generation Reference

When requesting AI-generated audio, include:

- Audio asset type and purpose.
- Relevant audio direction principles.
- Desired emotional tone.
- Musical or sound design style.
- Instrumentation or sound sources.
- Tempo and rhythm, where applicable.
- Duration.
- Looping requirements.
- Spatial and environmental characteristics.
- Technical constraints.
- Elements to include and avoid.

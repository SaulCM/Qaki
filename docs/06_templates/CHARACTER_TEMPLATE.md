# 15. AI-Assisted Character Development

AI tools may support character brainstorming, narrative exploration, concept art generation, dialogue drafting, and technical prototyping.

AI-assisted work must follow these requirements:

1. Use this document as the authoritative character reference.
2. Preserve established character identity, motivations, personality, and relationships.
3. Do not introduce contradictory character facts without an approved design change.
4. Do not invent narrative events or relationships as established facts.
5. Clearly distinguish approved character information from proposals.
6. Review generated dialogue for voice consistency and narrative accuracy.
7. Review generated visual and audio assets for artistic consistency and usage rights.
8. Do not make major changes to character identity or role without project owner approval.
9. Keep character-specific information within the appropriate project documentation.
10. Verify technical implementations against the project's architecture and coding standards.

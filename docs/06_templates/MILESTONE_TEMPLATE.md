## 12. AI-Assisted Milestone Management

AI agents assisting with milestone planning or tracking MUST:

1. Read the project backlog, relevant milestone records, and applicable production standards before proposing changes.
2. Preserve the milestone's approved objectives, scope, and acceptance criteria.
3. Distinguish actual progress from estimates and assumptions.
4. Never mark deliverables or acceptance criteria as complete without supporting evidence.
5. Identify dependencies, blockers, risks, and schedule impacts.
6. Avoid silently expanding or reducing the approved scope.
7. Propose changes with their expected impact on schedule, quality, and dependencies.
8. Keep milestone records consistent with actual project state.
9. Reference existing backlog items rather than creating duplicate work items.
10. Never mark a milestone as Completed or approve a significant scope change without project owner authorization.

AI-generated progress reports and completion assessments must be reviewed by the project owner.
